## Environments

An **environment** is a place to deliver into. Each has:

- a **stage** — dev, test, staging or prod — which policies can match on;
- a **type** — *shared* by many systems, *dedicated* to one, or *ephemeral*;
- an **environment blueprint**, which builds its landing zone;
- **outputs** it shares with everything that runs in it — a tracing endpoint, a log level, a network.

Environments are versioned like everything else: a change is a new version, reviewed as a draft first.

### Slots

An environment can be divided into **slots**, each described by dimensions such as `region=eu-west-1` or
`country=DE`. Use slots for data residency, latency, or anything else where one environment needs several
copies of the same workload. A slot can override the environment's outputs.

### Ephemeral environments

An ephemeral environment carries a **lease**. A pull-request preview expires on its own instead of running
up a bill until someone notices.

## Placement rules

A **placement rule** says which workloads may go into which environments — and, optionally, into which
slots at most. Rules match workloads by business domain, system, owner or blueprint type, and either
**allow** or **deny**. Deny is what makes "every domain except payments" expressible.

When a component asks to be placed somewhere, Lembos checks it against the rules before anything runs.

## Instances

Under **Platform → Instances**, every building block in every slot of every environment shows what it
should be running, what it was last seen running, and whether the two match.
