The Portal is organised into three areas, one for each kind of work:

| Area             | For                                   | Holds                                                                         |
|------------------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Workspace**    | Product teams                          | Business domains, software systems, components, APIs and resources           |
| **Platform**     | Platform, security and operations teams | Blueprints, environments, execution targets, source credentials, placement rules, policies, instances, artifacts and the audit log |
| **Organization** | Administrators                         | Users, groups, roles and service accounts                                     |

What you can see and change in each depends on your [roles and group memberships](/docs/use/access).

## A typical first week

1. **An administrator sets up the organisation** — groups for your teams, and people in them.
   [Access](/docs/use/access)
2. **The platform team registers where things run** — a cloud account or cluster as an execution target,
   and credentials for private sources. [Golden paths](/docs/use/golden-paths)
3. **The platform team publishes golden paths** — blueprints for a web service, a database, an
   environment. [Golden paths](/docs/use/golden-paths)
4. **The platform team declares environments** — dev, staging and production, with slots where production
   is split by region or country, and rules for what may be placed where.
   [Environments](/docs/use/environments)
5. **Product teams describe their software** — systems, components, APIs and the resources they need,
   each on a blueprint. [Catalog](/docs/use/catalog)
6. **Product teams ship** — configure each component, trigger a release and promote it from environment to
   environment. [Delivery](/docs/use/delivery)
7. **Guardrails go in as you learn** — policies that warn first, then enforce.
   [Governance](/docs/use/governance)
