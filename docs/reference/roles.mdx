title: Built-in roles
description: The roles Lembos ships with, and what each one may do.
---

Roles answer *may this principal do this kind of thing at all?* Whether they reach a **particular** thing
is decided by group ownership, and whether a particular change is allowed now by
[policy](/docs/use/governance). See [People and access](/docs/use/access).

## Roles for people

### Contributor

For the people who build and ship.

- **Catalog** — create, change and archive business domains, systems, components, APIs and resources.
- **Delivery** — request and manage runs; assemble releases and promote them.
- **Everything else** — read only: environments, placement rules, instances, blueprints, artifacts,
  execution targets, source credentials, policies and the audit log; users, groups and roles.

A Contributor cannot see the list of service accounts.

### Maintainer

For the platform team.

- **Infrastructure** — create, change and archive blueprints and execution targets; manage source
  credentials and artifacts, including quarantining an artifact.
- **Delivery** — create, change and archive environments and placement rules; **approve** runs; promote
  releases.
- **Governance** — create, change and archive policies; read the audit log.
- **Everything else** — read only, service accounts included.

A Maintainer approves other people's runs but cannot request runs of their own, so an approval always means
a second person agreed.

### Administrator

Everything a person can do on the platform, including inviting and managing users and managing groups,
roles and service accounts.

## Roles only machines hold

Three roles exist for Lembos's own background work and can never be given to a person:

| Role            | Held by                  | Does                                                            |
|-----------------|--------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Executor**    | The execution agent      | Runs provisioning and deployment work, using execution-target and source credentials |
| **Reconciler**  | The drift reconciler     | Reads what should be running and reports what is                |
| **Provisioner** | The directory synchronizer | Creates and retires accounts from your directory              |

Using a cloud account's credentials or a private source's secret are permissions only the Executor holds,
and every use is audited.

## Your own roles

Administrators can create roles from any combination of permissions — for instance, splitting "assemble a
release" from "promote it to production". Built-in roles cannot be renamed or archived.
